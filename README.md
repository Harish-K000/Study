## Start here

1. [Core Interview Script](01%20-%20Core%20Interview%20Script.md) — the final spoken-answer document. Revise this aloud; it is intentionally conversational rather than textbook-style.
2. [Resume Script](07-resume/RESUME-SCRIPT.md) — verify personal contribution and every metric.
3. [Behavioral Preparation](06-behavioral/README.md) — rehearse STAR stories aloud.
4. [Interlinked Question Map](08-question-maps/QUESTION-MAP.md) — practice likely follow-up paths.

The two older “final” books remain as reference material, but the **Core Interview Script is the source of truth for revision**. Topic folders provide deeper explanations and code.

Use the [Enterprise Java Interview Playbook](FINAL-INTERVIEW-HANDBOOK.md) as your primary document. It is written as a connected conversation with a senior interviewer. The [Final Interview Answer Book](FINAL-INTERVIEW-ANSWER-BOOK.md) remains available as a topic-by-topic revision guide.

This handbook converts the preparation discussions into concise, interview-ready answers. Each topic combines the definition, an intuitive analogy, a practical example, likely follow-ups, and code where useful.

## How to answer in the interview

Use this pattern:

1. Give the direct answer in one or two sentences.
2. Explain how it works technically.
3. Connect it to Cognizant or Siemens.
4. Stop and allow the interviewer to ask a follow-up.

Do not force an analogy into every answer. Use one when it makes a difficult idea clearer.

## Recommended study order

1. Read the [Enterprise Java Interview Playbook](FINAL-INTERVIEW-HANDBOOK.md) once from beginning to end.
2. Memorize the structure—not the exact wording—of the [resume script](07-resume/RESUME-SCRIPT.md).
3. Review [Spring Boot](02-spring-boot/README.md) and [Java core](01-java-core/README.md).
4. Review [database](03-database/README.md) and [AWS/DevOps](04-aws-devops/README.md).
5. Practise the [behavioral stories](06-behavioral/README.md) aloud.
6. Walk through the [flight-booking design](05-system-design/README.md).
7. Use the [question map](08-question-maps/QUESTION-MAP.md) to practise follow-up questions.

## Accuracy rule

Separate the overall platform from your personal contribution. Say “the platform used” for architecture-level components and “I implemented” only for work you personally performed. Do not claim a metric unless you can explain its baseline, measurement source, comparison period, and your contribution.
